🎯 Identify the Red Flags:

  • 🔴 Suspicious sender domain
  • 🔴 Urgency and threats
  • 🔴 Spelling/grammar errors
  • 🔴 Generic greeting
  • 🔴 Different reply-to address
  • 🔴 Sent at unusual time (3:42 AM)
