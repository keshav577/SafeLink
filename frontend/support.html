📋 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? ▼

To reset your password:

  1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password"
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Check your email for a reset link (may take up to 5 minutes)
  4. Click the link and create a new password
  5. Make sure your password is at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and numbers

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

How does the gamification system work? ▼

SafeLink uses gamification to make learning fun and engaging:

  • XP (Experience Points): Earned by completing lessons, quizzes, and simulations
  • Levels: Gain 1 level for every 500 XP earned
  • Badges: Unlock achievements for completing specific challenges
  • Streaks: Maintain daily learning to build your streak
  • Leaderboard: Compete with other learners globally

Track all your progress on the Dashboard!

What is adaptive learning? ▼

Adaptive learning personalizes your experience based on your performance:

  • If you score high on quizzes, you'll get more challenging content
  • If you struggle with a topic, we'll provide additional resources
  • Content difficulty adjusts automatically to match your skill level
  • You'll receive personalized recommendations based on your weaknesses

This ensures you're always learning at the optimal pace for you!

How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)? ▼

To enable 2FA for extra security:

  1. Go to Settings → Security
  2. Click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication"
  3. Download an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
  4. Scan the QR code with your app
  5. Enter the 6-digit code to verify
  6. Save your backup codes in a safe place

Important: Keep your backup codes secure. You'll need them if you lose access to your authenticator app.

Can I download lessons for offline viewing? ▼

Yes! Offline access is available for premium users:

  • Go to Settings → Data & Storage
  • Enable "Download Lessons for Offline"
  • Choose your preferred video quality
  • Download individual lessons from the learning hub

Downloaded content is available for 30 days and will sync with your progress when you're back online.

How are phishing simulations different from quizzes? ▼

Simulations provide hands-on, realistic practice:

  • Quizzes: Test theoretical knowledge with multiple-choice questions
  • Simulations: Interactive scenarios where you analyze real-world examples

In simulations, you'll examine actual phishing emails, analyze URLs, check sender information, and make decisions just like you would in real life. This practical experience is crucial for developing real-world security skills.

What should I do if I find a bug or error? ▼

We appreciate bug reports! Here's how to report:

  1. Click the "Report Issue" button below
  2. Describe what you were doing when the error occurred
  3. Include any error messages you saw
  4. Take a screenshot if possible
  5. Tell us your browser and device information

Our team typically responds within 24 hours. Critical bugs are addressed immediately.

Do you offer certificates of completion? ▼

Yes! You can earn certificates in two ways:

  • Course Completion: Complete all lessons in a learning path
  • Achievement Certificates: Reach specific milestones (e.g., 50 quizzes passed)

Certificates include:

  • Your name and achievement
  • Completion date
  • Unique verification code
  • ShareableLinkedIn integration

Find all your certificates in Settings → Data & Storage → Export Certificates
